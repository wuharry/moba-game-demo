Implementation
offline_ai/hero_decisions.lua is loaded by Action_AIHero_Common.lua. It keeps
the existing hero-specific spell scorers and native range/mana/cooldown checks.
It chooses higher-scoring skills first and tries another candidate after a
failed cast, bounded to three attempts and one successful dispatch per decision.
Checks occur every four action callbacks; callback duration has NOT been timed.
Positive hero-spell scores below the normal-attack weight55 remain eligible
every third decision against enemy heroes. This avoids starving mages such as
entry365 whose original scorers give modest scores even for useful attacks.
Weak summoner/recall scores and creep targets cannot use that fallback.
Typed target validation prevents positional payloads/channel enums being used
as object IDs. Existing channeling and retreat/recall are not interrupted.

Emergency retreat: enemy tower targets the bot, HP <=22%, HP <45% while locally
outnumbered, or HP <70% while outnumbered by at least two. Reengagement needs
HP >=50%, no tower aggro, and sufficient nearby allied heroes. The original
retreat paths/timers/base-defense behavior remain in use. Existing retreat
score now retains tower/target danger instead of overwriting it; two wrong
parameter reads are corrected to faction and HP.

Local focus keeps the original five-world-unit search. It favors vulnerable
enemy heroes, adds priority for the same enemy being attacked by a nearby ally,
and enemies pursuing allied heroes below45% HP. It avoids unsupported tower
targets and excessive low-HP pursuit. Native visibility/attackability queries
remain responsible for fog filtering. ForcePriorityChange sets a temporary
priority level; it does not cumulatively add values (native disassembly checked).
No combat-stat, cooldown, movement-speed, or hidden-vision advantages added.

Diagnostics and interpretation
The clone overwrites files/offline-ai-metrics.txt about every30 game seconds.
The pregame countdown resets to zero; reporting handles that backward clock.
The snapshot includes alive bots only. Absence can mean death, not missing AI.
casts = native API accepted dispatches (switch skills have no return value),
not confirmed hits. fallback = a later candidate accepted after a failed one.
assist = teamwork-based priority selections, not completed assists or kills.
retreats/tower = requests via the retained retreat machinery, not guarantees of
survival. errors = scorer exceptions; controlled tests intentionally inject one.
No win-rate or performance improvement has been measured. Short representative
matches do not cover every hero/skin, full victories, or long-session stability.
